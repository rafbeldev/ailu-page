# Privacy Policy for Ailu: Ensemble Weather

**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Ailu: Ensemble Weather** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. 

We believe that your privacy is a fundamental right. **Ailu: Ensemble Weather does not collect, track, sell, or share any of your personal data.**

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
* **Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or user IDs. No user account or registration is required to use the App.
* **Device Identifiers:** We do not access or collect device identifiers such as Android ID, IMEI, MAC address, advertising IDs (AAID), or Firebase installation IDs.
* **Device GPS Location:** The App does **not** request or use Android location permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`). We do not track or monitor your device's physical location.
* **Analytics & Telemetry:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel) to monitor your activity.
* **Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements and integrates no ad networks.

---

### 2. Information Handled by the App

#### A. Forecast & Search Queries (Open-Meteo API)
To provide meteorological charts and forecasts, the App connects to the **Open-Meteo API** (`https://open-meteo.com`):
* When you search for a city or enter geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude), these coordinates and forecast parameter requests are transmitted over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather models and geocoding results.
* No personal identity or device identifiers are attached to these weather queries.
* For more information on how Open-Meteo handles API requests, please review the [Open-Meteo Privacy Policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/features#privacy).

#### B. Local On-Device Storage
The App stores your chosen preferences locally on your device:
* Temperature and measurement units (°C/°F, km/h, mm, etc.).
* Chart display preferences, custom model colors, and line styles.
* App theme settings (Light/Dark/System).
* A local cache of recent locations and downloaded forecast series for fast, offline access.

**All of this data remains strictly on your device** and is never sent to our servers. You can permanently delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App.

---

### 3. Data Security
All communication between the App and external weather services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard **HTTPS (Transport Layer Security - TLS)**.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because we do not collect any personal data, the App is safe for general audiences of all ages.
