Privacy Policy

Ailu: Ensemble Weather • Last Updated: August 19, 2026
Privacy Summary: Ailu: Ensemble Weather does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or GPS sensor location data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, or account identifiers. No registration or account creation is required.
  • Device Identifiers: We do not collect or track hardware IDs, Android ID, IMEI, MAC addresses, Advertising IDs (AAID), or Firebase installation IDs.
  • Device GPS Location: The App does not request or use Android location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). We do not monitor your physical whereabouts.
  • Analytics & Telemetry: We do not embed any third-party analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • Advertisements: The App is ad-free and contains no advertising networks.

2. Information Handled by the App

A. Weather Queries & Geocoding (Open-Meteo API)

To provide meteorological forecast data, the App communicates with the Open-Meteo API:

  • When you search for a city or input latitude and longitude coordinates, these coordinates are sent securely over HTTPS to Open-Meteo to fetch model data.
  • No personally identifiable information or device IDs are attached to these network requests.
  • For further details, please refer to the Open-Meteo Privacy Policy.

B. Local On-Device Storage

The App stores your preferences locally on your device storage (via Android DataStore and SQLite):

  • Measurement unit preferences (°C/°F, km/h, mm, etc.).
  • Custom chart appearance, model colors, and line styles.
  • Theme settings (Light/Dark/System Default).
  • Local cache of recent location searches and forecast data for offline access.

This data never leaves your device and can be removed at any time by clearing the App's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Data Security

All network communications between the App and the Open-Meteo API are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (Transport Layer Security - TLS).

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from children or any other users. It is fully suitable and safe for all age groups.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
